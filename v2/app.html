§2 Compensation · classification
The Employee is classified as exempt from overtime under applicable law.
The Employee is classified as non-exempt. Hours over 8 in a day or 40 in a week are paid at 1.5× under California Labor Code §510.
Why: at $45/hour part-time the salary threshold for exemption is not met; misclassification is the most common claim in CA.
§4 Working hours · new clause
—
4.3 Meal and rest periods. For shifts over five hours the Employee receives an unpaid 30-minute meal period and a paid 10-minute rest period per four hours worked, per Labor Code §512 and IWC Order 4.
Why: required for non-exempt employees; missing language is a penalty exposure of one hour's pay per violation.
§9 Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason with thirty (30) days' notice.
Employment is at will. Either party may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice. The Company will pay all earned wages at termination as required by Labor Code §201–203.
Why: the notice clause contradicts the at-will statement in §1 and creates an implied-contract risk.
