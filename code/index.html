How it works

The Claude Code experience you know, with one difference: every step is a vote.

1

Connect a repo, assign a task

Sign in with GitHub, pick a repository, and describe the work in plain words. Bugs, features, tests, migrations. Attach screenshots if it helps.

2

Three models run at once

Each works in its own sandbox on a copy of your branch and runs your test suite. Names are hidden so you judge the diff, not the brand.

3

Pick one, or fix it yourself

Apply the result you want and we open the PR. If none is right, edit the diff; your fix is the record the models learn from most.

Why it’s free

Your choices are worth more than a subscription. AI labs pay for honest, side-by-side judgements on real tasks, and for the fixes models miss. That pays for your inference.

What we record

The task, the three diffs, the test results, which one you applied, your reason, and the next thing you asked for. Overrides keep your diff and note.

What we never record

Your name, email or repository name. Repository contents stay out unless you turn them on. Private repos are never recorded. Secrets are always redacted.

Your controls

Switch any record type off, export everything, or delete it. Opting out does not change what you get: three models, free.
