Data & settings

What PowerHouse records

PowerHouse Code is free because your choices train better models. Here is exactly what is kept, and what never is.

Preference recordsThe task, the three diffs, which one you applied, your reason chips and note, and the test results.
Override recordsWhen you reject all three, your diff and note are stored as the human fix.
Next-step signalYour follow-up prompt after a choice is stored as feedback on what the chosen result still missed.
Include repository contentsOff: only diffs, file paths and the task are kept. On: the files the models read are kept too, which makes the record more useful. Secrets are always redacted.
Only record on public repositoriesPrivate repos still get three models; nothing from them is stored.
Records are anonymised before they leave PowerHouse and are sold to AI labs in bulk. No record carries your name, email or repository name. Read the data terms
Records already delivered to a lab cannot be recalled.
